# DatcampV2_design_portofolio
Portofolio Design as a Coordinator of the PDD Division for DatCamp vol 2

As the Coordinator of the PDD Division for Datcamp Vol 2, I led the planning and execution of visual design, publication materials, and event documentation for both the Data Science Webinar and the Datcamp Vol 2 Bootcamp.

I directed the production of digital assets and promotional content, ensuring that complex details such as speaker highlights, webinar agendas, and bootcamp schedules were presented clearly and engagingly. I also managed documentation activities and collaborated with other divisions to maintain consistent branding and ensure timely communication with participants throughout both events.

This role strengthened my skills in team leadership, project coordination, visual communication, UI/UX thinking, and content organization, particularly in transforming event workflows into structured, user-friendly digital visuals.
